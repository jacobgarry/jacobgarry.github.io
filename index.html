About Me

Engineering through analysis and testing.

I am an aerospace engineering student at North Carolina State University focused on the design, integration, and experimental validation of multidisciplinary aerospace systems.

My work spans high-altitude flight hardware, compressible-flow analysis, embedded instrumentation, control systems, aerodynamic testing, CAD, CFD, and additive manufacturing.

I enjoy carrying projects from analytical modeling and mechanical design through fabrication, system integration, testing, data reduction, and technical documentation.

Engineering Projects

Selected projects spanning high-altitude flight systems, compressible-flow design, embedded instrumentation, wind-tunnel testing, and active aerodynamic control.

PROJECT 01

NASA HASP Scientific Balloon Payload

Integrated and tested a high-altitude scientific payload designed to compare radiation effects on two NVIDIA Jetson computers, including one protected by a tungsten-filled conformal coating. Supported power, thermal, communication, software, and system-level testing through NASA integration.

PROJECT 02

Convergent-Divergent Nozzle Design and Testing

Designed, manufactured, and experimentally tested a 3D-printed convergent-divergent nozzle. Compared one-dimensional isentropic predictions with ANSYS CFD and wind-tunnel Pitot measurements to evaluate choking, supersonic acceleration, probe blockage, and overexpanded exit behavior.

Compressible Flow ANSYS CFD SolidWorks Wind-Tunnel Testing
View Project
PROJECT 03

Automated Wind-Tunnel Measurement System

Developed an automated measurement rail for collecting airflow data across a fan-array test section. Integrated a stepper motor, distance sensor, differential-pressure sensor, limit switch, and ESP32-based control system to execute repeatable spatial measurements.

ESP32 Automation Instrumentation Wind-Tunnel Testing
PROJECT 04

Active Airbrake System for High-Powered Rocketry

Contributed to the design, fabrication, integration, and testing of an actively controlled aerodynamic airbrake system for competition rockets. The project combined mechanical design, embedded control, servo actuation, flight instrumentation, and post-flight data analysis to improve apogee accuracy and system reliability.
